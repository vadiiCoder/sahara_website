## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Hero Section
- Stunning introduction to the Sahara
- Navigation bar with smooth section links
- Highlight cards for famous desert locations

### 🌍 Regions Section
- Featured desert destinations:
  - Moroccan Sahara (Erg Chebbi)
  - Tunisian Sahara (Douz & Chott el Djerid)
  - Egyptian White Desert
  - Algerian Sahara (Tassili n’Ajjer)
- Image-rich cards with descriptions

### 📖 About Section
- Company story and mission
- Image slideshow with navigation arrows
- Smooth fade transitions using JavaScript

### 🔥 Explore Section
- Key travel benefits:
  - Expert guides
  - Authentic experiences
  - Sustainable travel
  - Tailored journeys

### 📩 Contact Section
- Fully structured contact form
- Social media integration
- Business contact information

### 🦶 Footer
- Quick navigation links
- Newsletter subscription form
- Social media icons
