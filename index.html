Our Featured Regions

Moroccan Sahara: Erg Chebbi

Venture into the towering dunes of Erg Chebbi, near Merzouga. Experience camel treks, spend nights in traditional Berber camps under a sky full of stars, and witness breathtaking desert sunrises and sunsets.

Tunisian Sahara: Douz & Chott el Djerid

Explore Douz, the 'Gateway to the Sahara,' and traverse the vast salt lake of Chott el Djerid. Discover ancient Roman ruins, palm groves, and traditional desert life.

Egyptian Sahara: White Desert

Journey to the otherworldly White Desert, famous for its unique chalk rock formations sculpted by wind. This region offers incredible stargazing and a truly surreal landscape.

Algerian Sahara: Tassili n'Ajjer

A UNESCO World Heritage site, Tassili n'Ajjer is a vast plateau in the Algerian Sahara known for its prehistoric cave art and unique geological formations, offering a journey back in time.

Our Story: Journeys Beyond Horizons

Born from a deep love for the vast, golden expanse of the Sahara, our company was founded to share its unparalleled beauty and mystery with the world. We believe that an adventure here is more than just a trip; it's a transformative experience.

For years, we've meticulously crafted itineraries that go beyond the ordinary, ensuring every journey is rich with authentic cultural encounters, breathtaking landscapes, and moments of profound tranquility. Our expert guides, often local to the regions we explore, are not just navigators but storytellers, sharing the secrets of the desert's history, geology, and traditions.
